## What the creators share

> Numbered list, 6-10 items. These are the closest thing to laws in your corpus —
> the things true of every video regardless of who made it. Think about: what
> determines which visual appears, how many ideas a single composition carries,
> what returning to the creator's face accomplishes, where proof sits relative
> to the claim it supports, how many distinct text systems are running at once,
> what motivates a transition, how fast the hook becomes legible, and how the
> CTA differs in energy from the rest.
